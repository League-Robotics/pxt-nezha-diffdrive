NOTE ON THE FLASH: the first TWO attempts via ~/bin/mbflash FAILED
with DAPLink FAIL.TXT "error: The transfer timed out. type: transient,
user", and left the board with NO working program -- silent, but dead.
Cause: mbflash mounts the MICROBIT volume with the `flush` option
(synchronous writes), which on the Pi Zero 2 W's USB is too slow for
DAPLink's transfer window. Fix: mount WITHOUT flush and sync after:
    sudo mount -o uid=$(id -u) /dev/sda /mnt/microbit
    cp hex /mnt/microbit/    # 0.08 s buffered
    sync                     # 34.7 s, completes cleanly
Silence after a failed flash is INDISTINGUISHABLE from correct
reply-only behavior. Always prove liveness with HELLO/RUN:probe.
